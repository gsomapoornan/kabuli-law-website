## Content and imagery
Branding and marketing language are proposed for review. Practice-area descriptions and topic lists are illustrative draft content for the firm to confirm; service dialogs provide questions to discuss. No invented testimonials, ratings, qualifications, languages, experience statistics, phone number, email, fees, or office hours.

Built-in ImageGen created dist/assets/architecture.png. It is generic architecture, explicitly labelled; it does not represent the office. The N/K profile composition is typography, not a substitute portrait. Final image prompt:

“Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium law website hero, landscape architectural photograph. A beautifully composed close-up of contemporary limestone courthouse architecture, tall rhythmic stone columns and deep recessed dark bronze windows, warm late-afternoon sunlight casting long precise shadows, a small hint of olive foliage at lower right, quiet dignified atmosphere. Sophisticated editorial architectural photography, realistic stone grain, rich charcoal shadows, warm ivory highlights, subtle film texture, restrained neutral palette. Low angle, strong vertical lines, composition fills entire frame. No people, no signage, no text, no logos. This is generic conceptual architecture, not a depiction of any actual firm's office.”

Before an official launch, the firm should review copy, service scope, branding, contact channels, biography, image choices, and legal notices. Google Fonts are loaded externally with local fallback fonts.


## November 2026 design update

The refreshed layout takes structural inspiration from https://maranilaw.com/ with original copy, the existing teal/cream/gold palette, and Kabuli Law’s four established practice areas. Four new AI photographs are now used for real estate, wills and estates, family, and business sections, as well as the hero and preparation guides. See IMAGE-NOTES.md for asset paths and prompts. The original architecture image remains in the repository but is no longer referenced. No unverified testimonials, awards, or credentials were added.

JavaScript syntax, element IDs, section targets, and local asset presence were checked. Automated visual/browser verification was blocked by browser socket restrictions in this environment; desktop and mobile visual review remains necessary.
